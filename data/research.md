Thesis Title:
Vision-Language Model–Based Filipino Sign Language Recognition
for Real-Time Text and Speech Translation on Raspberry Pi

Abstract:
This research focuses on developing an AI system capable of
recognizing Filipino Sign Language gestures in real time using computer vision.

Methods:
- Camera-based gesture capture
- Vision-language modeling
- Embedded system optimization

Contribution:
Provides a low-cost, accessible solution for sign language translation.

Thesis Title:
PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT OF DYE-SENSITIZED SOLAR CELL 
BETWEEN Cu-C-TiO2 AND TiO2 EXTRACTED FROM MANGOSTEEN
(GARCINIA MANGOSTANA) PERICARP

Abstract:
This study aimed to compare the performance of absorption rate 
between dye-sensitized solar cell with the Cu-C-TiO2 and dye
sensitized solar cell with the TiO2 with Mangosteen as their dye 
sensitizer.
